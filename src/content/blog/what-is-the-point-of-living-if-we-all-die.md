Why do we keep going even though we know that someday we will die and all the things we do will be futile or  will go in vain. From birth to death, we are programmed to do what is expected of us. A man spends his entire youth in search of a good job, then he is compelled to lead a family life while continuing his bloodline. A woman hustles from being a teen to an adult lover to a wife then a mother and all of this for someday to just die. A billionaire lives his entire life to its fullest or saves and earns the money to spend at old age just to die at the end, the rich don’t get to take their wealth after death and the poor still doesn’t get respect and lifestyle after all the hardships they have been through, merely to be cremated or buried in the earth at the end. To me the sheer point of life is just POINTLESS, when I say this, I truly believe that we are intelligent beings and our species, at this stage of its existence is just here to maintain social norms at its regulated parameters and margins. **Something ending doesn't necessarily make it meaningless.** A song ends. A sunset ends. Childhood ends. A conversation ends. Yet we don't necessarily conclude that those things were pointless. But - 

**Why does the breeze blow, Why does the sky sway?**

**Why does the heart stir, Neither you know, nor do I.**

This verse may sound romantic for some, its because this is a romantic Bollywood song named ‘Na Tum Jano Hum’, While writing this piece the song just happened to me and I translated the first stanza, realizing that it entirely represents what I am trying to blabber since I started writing this that is Neither you nor I know what we are, what we are supposed to do, to be! and even if someone finds their calling and the ultimate purpose of their life, still question remains is that what they leave behind?

I am inclined to think that we are a species meant to achieve more than merely living and dying within the limits of our biology. Perhaps the point of life is not to escape death, but to make our existence mean something beyond it. Maybe we are not meant to remain confined to the conditions we were born into, but to evolve beyond them, intellectually, technologically, and perhaps even existentially. Maybe we are not here merely to live within the boundaries handed down to us, but to question them. To create, discover, love, and transcend the limits of what we believe we are capable of. Because if we are destined to die, then perhaps the question is not **why we die**, but **what we leave behind before we do.**
