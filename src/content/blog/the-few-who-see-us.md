> You are lucky if you have a person who listens,
>
> You are lucky if someone knows the real you.
>
> When you look at them, somehow your eyes glisten
>
> A feeling reserved for a select few.
>
> Like the moon that travels around the Earth,
>
> They find their way back, only to you;
>
> And somehow, they remind you of your worth
>
> When even you have forgotten it too.
>
> They hear the words that you never quite say,
>
> They notice the silence hidden inside your voice.
>
> And when the world seems to turn away,
>
> They stay; not from duty but from choice.
>
> You need not pretend, you need not explain,
>
> Nor hide scars behind a weird smile.
>
> They hold your hand through the night and pain,
>
> They don’t need to, yet they go the extra mile.
>
> So, if you have someone who sees you this way,
>
> Hold them close, but let them remain free;
>
> Let them know, and let them feel, the love you hold for them.
>
> For among all the people we meet every day,
>
> Few will know us and still choose to see.
